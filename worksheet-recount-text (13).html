Stage 1

Introduction to Recount Text

Learn the core concept, social function, and categories of Recount Text in English.

What is Recount Text?

A Recount Text is a type of English text that retells past events, experiences, or activities in chronological order (the order in which they happened).

💡 Key Rule: Recount Text always retells actions that ALREADY happened in the past.

Social Function (Purpose)

The main goals of writing a Recount Text include:

  • To entertain or amuse readers with personal stories.
  • To inform readers about what happened and when.
  • To record historical events or memorable moments.

4 Major Types of Recount Text

1. Personal Recount

Retells author's personal experiences (e.g., My Holiday in Bali).

2. Factual Recount

Reports factual incidents (e.g., Police report, science experiment).

3. Biographical Recount

Recounts a famous person's life history (e.g., Life of B.J. Habibie).

4. Imaginative Recount

Applies imaginary details to a real situation (e.g., A Day as an Astronaut).

1

Orientation (Pengenalan)

Paragraf Pembuka

Berisi pengenalan tentang SIAPA (Who) yang terlibat, DI MANA (Where) kejadian berlangsung, dan KAPAN (When) peristiwa itu terjadi.

Example: Last month, my family and I went to Pangandaran Beach for holiday. We stayed at a small hotel near the coast.
2

Events (Urutan Peristiwa)

Isi / Inti Cerita

Menceritakan serangkaian kejadian secara kronologis dari awal hingga akhir. Biasanya terdiri dari beberapa paragraf dan menggunakan kata penghubung waktu (First, Then, After that, Finally).

Example: On the first day, we built sandcastles and played volleyball. The next morning, we rented a boat to explore the small island...
3

Re-orientation (Kesan / Penutup)

Paragraf Penutup (Opsional)

Berisi rangkuman, kesimpulan, atau kesan/perasaan pribadi (personal comment) penulis terhadap peristiwa tersebut.

Example: It was the best vacation ever! We felt very tired but extremely happy.

[Orientation] Last Sunday, my school organized a trip to the National Museum in Jakarta. Thirty students and two teachers joined the trip. We departed at 7.00 AM by bus.

[Events] When we arrived at 9.00 AM, a tour guide welcomed us. First, we walked into the historical gallery and saw ancient statues. Next, we observed traditional weapons and clothes from various provinces. After having lunch together at noon, we watched a short documentary film about Indonesian independence history.

[Re-orientation] Finally, at 4.00 PM, we went back home. Overall, it was a very educational and enjoyable trip. I learned a lot about our nation's history.

Stage 3

Language Features (Unsur Kebahasaan)

Pendalaman Simple Past Tense, Regular/Irregular Verbs, dan Time Connectors.

1

Simple Past Tense

Menggunakan kata kerja bentuk kedua (Verb 2) untuk menyatakan tindakan yang telah selesai di masa lampau.

Subject + Verb 2 + Object
2

Chronological Connectors

Kata penghubung waktu untuk menunjukkan urutan peristiwa secara logis dan runtut.

First Then After that Finally
3

Adverbs of Time & Place

Keterangan waktu dan tempat yang spesifik di masa lampau.

  • Yesterday, last week, 2 days ago
  • In Yogyakarta, at the beach

Regular vs Irregular Verbs Cheat-Sheet

Regular Verbs (+ed / +d)

visit → visited
play → played
walk → walked
cook → cooked
study → studied
arrive → arrived

Irregular Verbs (Berubah Bentuk)

go → went
see → saw
buy → bought
eat → ate
take → took
make → made

Historical Recount Sample

The Proclamation of Indonesian Independence

The Proclamation of Indonesian Independence occurred on Friday, 17 August 1945. The declaration marked the start of the diplomatic and armed resistance of the Indonesian National Revolution, fighting against the forces of the Netherlands.

First, in the early morning of August 17, Ir. Soekarno and Drs. Mohammad Hatta gathered with other freedom fighters at Jalan Pegangsaan Timur No. 56, Jakarta. They drafted the concise text of the proclamation. Sayuti Melik then typed the final draft of the proclamation text.

At 10:00 AM, Ir. Soekarno read the proclamation text in front of a crowd. After the reading, the red-and-white Indonesian flag was raised for the first time, accompanied by the national anthem "Indonesia Raya".

This historic event was brief but held immense significance for all Indonesian people, as it marked the birth of a free nation.
