What is Descriptive Text & Multimodal Text?

What is Descriptive Text?

A Descriptive Text describes a specific person, athlete, place, or thing in detail so that readers can visualize them clearly.

When Describing Athletes: We depict their physical appearance (height, muscular build, hair), personality traits (disciplined, fearless), and special achievements.
Full Screen Mode

What is Multimodal Text? (Poster)

A Multimodal Text combines two or more modes of communication:

  • Visual Mode: Character illustrations, photos, icons, and infographics.
  • Written Mode: Descriptive captions, labels, callouts, and bullet points.
  • Spatial Layout: Strategic arrangement of titles, stats, and character cards.
Full Screen Mode

Generic Structure of Descriptive Text

PART 1

1. Identification (Identifikasi)

Identifies the specific person/athlete to be described. Answers: Who is this person? What is their sport or nationality?

Example: "Veddriq Leonardo is a world-class Indonesian speed climber born in Pontianak."
PART 2

2. Description (Deskripsi)

Describes physical appearance, personality traits, skills, habits, and notable achievements in detail.

Example: "He has a muscular frame and sharp focus. He holds speed climbing world records."
