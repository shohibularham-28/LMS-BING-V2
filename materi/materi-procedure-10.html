What is Procedure Text?

A Procedure Text gives instructions on how to do or make something through a sequence of actions or steps.

Social Function (Tujuan Sosial): To describe how something is accomplished through a sequence of actions or steps (e.g., maintaining a daily healthy routine or making a nutrient-rich smoothie).
Full Screen Mode

Key Language Features

  • Imperative Verbs: Command words placed at step starts (e.g., Drink, Mix, Pour, Limit, Blend).
  • Temporal Conjunctions: Sequence connectors (e.g., First, Next, Then, After that, Finally).
  • Adverbs of Manner/Time: Precision details (e.g., for 2 minutes, thoroughly, gently, cold).
Full Screen Mode

The 3 Essential Parts of Procedure Text

PART 1

1. Goal / Aim (Tujuan)

States what is to be made or achieved. Usually reflected in the title.

e.g. "How to Maintain a Healthy Lifestyle"
PART 2

2. Materials / Ingredients

Lists items, tools, equipment, or food ingredients required.

e.g. "1 frozen banana, high-speed blender, water bottle"
PART 3

3. Steps / Instructions

Sequence of chronological actions start with imperative verbs.

e.g. "1. Drink 300 ml water. 2. Blend ingredients..."
