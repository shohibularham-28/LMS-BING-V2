1. Concept & Fundamentals

Understanding what Recount Text is and why we write it.

Full Screen Mode

What is Recount Text?

A Recount Text is a text that retells events, experiences, or actions that took place in the PAST.

Key Characteristic: Events are told in chronological order (in the sequence they actually happened).
Full Screen Mode

Social Function (Purpose)

The main purpose of a recount text is:

  • To inform readers about an event that occurred.
  • To entertain readers through engaging personal stories.
  • To record historical milestones or prominent biographies.
Full Screen Mode

Types of Recount Text

Personal Recount

Tells about personal experiences of the writer (e.g., My Trip to Yogyakarta).

Historical Recount

Records important historical events in the past (e.g., The Battle of Surabaya).

Biographical Recount

Recounts the life story or achievements of a famous person (e.g., B.J. Habibie).

Factual Recount

Reports details of particular incidents factually (e.g., Traffic Accident Report).

Full Screen Mode

Generic Structure of Recount Text

STEP 1

Orientation

Gives the background information. Answers Who was involved, Where it took place, and When it happened.

STEP 2

Events

A series of paragraphs telling what happened in chronological order (Event 1, Event 2, Event 3...).

STEP 3

Re-orientation

(Optional) A closing statement, personal impression, or summary of how the writer felt about the experience.
