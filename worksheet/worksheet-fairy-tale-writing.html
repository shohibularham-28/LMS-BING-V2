👤 Identitas Pengerjaan

🔮 Stage 1 — Match the words

Tap a word on the left, then tap its meaning on the right.

🪄 Stage 2 — Make sentences

Tap the words in the right order to make simple present sentences, positive and negative. Tap a word in the answer box to remove it.

📜 Stage 3 — Build the paragraph

These sentences tell one fairy tale, but they are mixed up. Use the arrows to put them in the right order.

🐉 Bonus — Write your own

Write 3 sentences of your own fairy tale in simple present tense. Use at least 1 negative sentence and 1 word from Stage 1. Your teacher will read this part.
