    🔢 Activity 5 — Put the Story in Order! 12 min
    Instructions: The story "The Frog Who Refused to be a Prince" below is scrambled. Tap the cards in the correct order (1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th). Tap a card again to undo it.
    – A. A princess found a talking frog by the pond and asked him to become a prince.
    – B. In the end, the princess and the frog became best friends, and the frog stayed a frog happily.
    – C. The frog said no, because he actually loved being a frog and did not want to change.
    – D. The princess was confused — no fairytale frog had ever said no before.

    Bonus discussion: Which cards belong to the complication part? Write the letters:

    Order: A → C → D → B. Complication: cards C & D

    🏷️ Activity 7 — Categorize the Type of Change 12 min
    Instructions: Each idea below is an example of one type of "fracture". Choose the correct category: Setting, Point of View, Character Role, or Ending.
    1. The story now happens in a modern high school instead of a castle.
    2. The tale is narrated by the wolf explaining his side of the story.
    3. The evil stepmother is actually trying to protect Cinderella from danger.
    4. Instead of getting married, the princess opens her own bakery.
    5. The kingdom is now set on a spaceship traveling to Mars.
    6. The dragon narrates how he only wanted to be left alone.
    7. The three pigs turn out to be con artists tricking the wolf.
    8. The frog stays a frog forever and becomes a famous singer instead of a prince.

    📝 Activity 11 — Write the Opening Paragraph 15 min
    Instructions: Based on your plot map, write the orientation paragraph (5-7 sentences) in English. Take turns — one sentence by A, one sentence by B.

    Checklist before you finish:

    • Includes an introduction of the main character
    • Includes a setting introduction (time & place)
    • Feels "fractured" / different from the original fairytale
    • Uses Simple Past Tense correctly
